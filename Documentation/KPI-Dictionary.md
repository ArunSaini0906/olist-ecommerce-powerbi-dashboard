# KPI Dictionary — Olist E-Commerce Business Intelligence Dashboard

This document defines the key performance indicators (KPIs) used in the
Olist E-Commerce Power BI dashboard.

The KPI framework is designed to measure commercial performance, customer
behavior, product performance, customer satisfaction, and operational
efficiency.

---

## 1. Commercial Performance KPIs

### Total Revenue

**Definition:**  
Total merchandise revenue generated from order items.

**Calculation:**

Total Revenue = SUM(Order Items[Revenue])

**Unit:**  
Currency (R$)

**Business Meaning:**  
Measures the overall merchandise sales generated by the business.

**Business Question:**  
How much revenue has the business generated?

**Used In:**
- Executive Overview
- Sales & Revenue Analysis
- Product & Category Performance
- Business Insights & Recommendations

---

### Total Orders

**Definition:**  
Number of unique orders placed by customers.

**Calculation:**

Total Orders = DISTINCTCOUNT(Orders[order_id])

**Unit:**  
Count

**Business Meaning:**  
Measures overall transaction volume.

**Business Question:**  
How many orders has the business processed?

---

### Average Order Value (AOV)

**Definition:**  
Average merchandise revenue generated per order.

**Calculation:**

AOV = Total Revenue / Total Orders

**Unit:**  
Currency (R$)

**Business Meaning:**  
Measures the average monetary value of each order.

**Business Question:**  
How much does the average customer spend per order?

**Interpretation:**
- Higher AOV → customers are spending more per transaction.
- Lower AOV → opportunity for cross-selling, bundling, or upselling.

---

### Revenue Growth %

**Definition:**  
Percentage change in revenue compared with the previous period.

**Calculation:**

Revenue Growth % =
(Current Period Revenue - Previous Period Revenue)
/
Previous Period Revenue

**Unit:**  
Percentage

**Business Meaning:**  
Measures whether the business is growing or declining over time.

**Business Question:**  
Is revenue increasing or decreasing?

**Important:**  
The exact comparison period depends on the selected date context.

---

### Order Growth %

**Definition:**  
Percentage change in order volume compared with the previous period.

**Calculation:**

Order Growth % =
(Current Period Orders - Previous Period Orders)
/
Previous Period Orders

**Unit:**  
Percentage

**Business Meaning:**  
Measures growth or decline in transaction volume.

---

## 2. Customer KPIs

### Total Customers

**Definition:**  
Number of unique customers in the dataset.

**Calculation:**

Total Customers =
DISTINCTCOUNT(Customers[customer_unique_id])

**Unit:**  
Count

**Business Meaning:**  
Measures the size of the customer base.

**Business Question:**  
How many unique customers have purchased from the business?

---

### Repeat Customers

**Definition:**  
Number of customers who placed more than one order.

**Calculation Logic:**

A customer is classified as a repeat customer when
their number of orders is greater than one.

**Unit:**  
Count

**Business Meaning:**  
Measures the number of customers who returned to purchase again.

---

### Repeat Customer Rate

**Definition:**  
Percentage of customers who made more than one purchase.

**Calculation:**

Repeat Customer Rate =
Repeat Customers / Total Customers

**Unit:**  
Percentage

**Business Meaning:**  
Measures customer retention and repeat-purchase behavior.

**Business Question:**  
How successful is the business at getting customers to purchase again?

**Business Interpretation:**  
A low repeat customer rate represents an opportunity to improve:
- Loyalty programs
- Personalization
- Cross-selling
- Remarketing
- Post-purchase engagement

---

### Average Customer Spend

**Definition:**  
Average historical merchandise revenue generated per unique customer.

**Calculation:**

Average Customer Spend =
Total Revenue / Total Customers

**Unit:**  
Currency (R$)

**Business Meaning:**  
Measures the average monetary value generated by each customer.

---

### Orders per Customer

**Definition:**  
Average number of orders generated per customer.

**Calculation:**

Orders per Customer =
Total Orders / Total Customers

**Unit:**  
Orders per customer

**Business Meaning:**  
Provides an indication of customer purchasing frequency.

---

### Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

**Definition:**  
Estimated total historical revenue generated by a customer.

**Calculation Logic:**

Customer Lifetime Value =
Total historical revenue generated by the customer.

**Important:**  
This project uses a historical customer-value approach rather than a
forward-looking predictive CLV model.

**Business Meaning:**  
Identifies customers who have generated higher historical value.

---

## 3. Customer Satisfaction KPIs

### Average Rating

**Definition:**  
Average customer review score.

**Calculation:**

Average Rating =
AVERAGE(Reviews[review_score])

**Unit:**  
Rating out of 5

**Business Meaning:**  
Measures overall customer satisfaction.

**Business Question:**  
How satisfied are customers with their purchases?

---

### Rating by Category

**Definition:**  
Average customer review score for each product category.

**Calculation:**

Average Rating by Category =
AVERAGE(Reviews[review_score])

**Business Meaning:**  
Identifies categories with strong or weak customer satisfaction.

**Business Question:**  
Which categories have customer experience problems?

---

## 4. Delivery & Operations KPIs

### Average Delivery Days

**Definition:**  
Average number of days between order purchase and customer delivery.

**Calculation Logic:**

Delivery Days =
Delivered Date - Purchase Date

Average Delivery Days =
AVERAGE(Delivery Days)

**Unit:**  
Days

**Business Meaning:**  
Measures overall delivery speed.

**Business Question:**  
How long does it take to deliver orders to customers?

---

### On-Time Delivery %

**Definition:**  
Percentage of delivered orders that arrived on or before the estimated
delivery date.

**Calculation:**

On-Time Delivery % =
On-Time Delivered Orders /
Total Delivered Orders

**Unit:**  
Percentage

**Business Meaning:**  
Measures logistics reliability and customer delivery experience.

**Business Question:**  
How reliably is the business meeting promised delivery dates?

---

### Late Delivery %

**Definition:**  
Percentage of delivered orders that arrived after the estimated
delivery date.

**Calculation:**

Late Delivery % =
Late Delivered Orders /
Total Delivered Orders

**Unit:**  
Percentage

**Business Meaning:**  
Measures delivery failures and operational risk.

**Business Question:**  
What percentage of delivered orders were late?

---

### Average Delivery Delay Days

**Definition:**  
Average number of days by which late deliveries exceeded the estimated
delivery date.

**Calculation Logic:**

Delivery Delay Days =
Actual Delivery Date - Estimated Delivery Date

Average Delivery Delay Days =
AVERAGE(Delay Days for late orders)

**Unit:**  
Days

**Business Meaning:**  
Measures the severity of late deliveries.

**Business Question:**  
When an order is late, how late is it?

---

## 5. Shipping & Cost KPI

### Freight Cost

**Definition:**  
Total freight value associated with order items.

**Calculation:**

Freight Cost =
SUM(Order Items[Freight Value])

**Unit:**  
Currency (R$)

**Business Meaning:**  
Measures the total shipping cost represented in the dataset.

---

### Freight-to-Revenue %

**Definition:**  
Freight cost as a percentage of merchandise revenue.

**Calculation:**

Freight-to-Revenue % =
Total Freight Cost / Total Revenue

**Unit:**  
Percentage

**Business Meaning:**  
Measures the relative importance of shipping costs compared with
merchandise revenue.

**Business Question:**  
How significant are freight costs relative to merchandise sales?

---

## 6. Product & Category KPIs

### Category Revenue

**Definition:**  
Total merchandise revenue generated by a product category.

**Calculation:**

Category Revenue =
SUM(Order Items[Revenue])

**Business Meaning:**  
Identifies categories that contribute most to revenue.

---

### Revenue Contribution %

**Definition:**  
Percentage of total revenue generated by a category.

**Calculation:**

Revenue Contribution % =
Category Revenue / Total Revenue

**Unit:**  
Percentage

**Business Meaning:**  
Measures the importance of each category to overall business revenue.

**Business Question:**  
Which categories contribute the most to the business?

---

### Category AOV

**Definition:**  
Average order value associated with a product category.

**Calculation:**

Category AOV =
Category Revenue / Category Orders

**Unit:**  
Currency (R$)

**Business Meaning:**  
Identifies categories with higher or lower order values.

---

### Top Product by Revenue

**Definition:**  
Product generating the highest merchandise revenue.

**Business Meaning:**  
Identifies the strongest individual products from a revenue perspective.

---

### Bottom Product by Revenue

**Definition:**  
Product generating relatively low merchandise revenue.

**Business Meaning:**  
Helps identify products that may require pricing, marketing,
inventory, assortment, or seller-level review.

---

## 7. Geographic KPIs

### Revenue by State

**Definition:**  
Total merchandise revenue generated from customers in each state.

**Calculation:**

Revenue by State =
SUM(Order Items[Revenue])

**Business Meaning:**  
Identifies geographic markets generating the highest business value.

---

### State Revenue Contribution %

**Definition:**  
Percentage of total revenue generated by a state.

**Calculation:**

State Revenue Contribution % =
State Revenue / Total Revenue

**Business Meaning:**  
Measures geographic concentration.

**Business Question:**  
Which geographic markets are most important to the business?

---

### Late Delivery Rate by State

**Definition:**  
Percentage of delivered orders in a state that were delivered late.

**Calculation:**

Late Delivery Rate =
Late Orders / Delivered Orders

**Business Meaning:**  
Identifies geographic logistics problems.

# KPI Interpretation Framework

KPIs should not be interpreted independently.

Management should evaluate them together.

For example:

| KPI Combination | Possible Interpretation |
|---|---|
| High Revenue + High Rating | Strong category/product |
| High Revenue + Low Rating | Commercially important but customer-experience risk |
| Low Revenue + High Rating | Potential growth opportunity |
| Low Revenue + Low Rating | Candidate for review |
| High Revenue + High Late Delivery | Operational risk |
| Low Repeat Rate + High Customer Base | Customer retention opportunity |
| High Freight % + Low AOV | Potential shipping-cost problem |

---

# KPI Design Principles

The dashboard follows these principles:

1. KPIs must have a clear business definition.
2. KPIs should be calculated from reliable source fields.
3. Measures are preferred over unnecessary calculated columns.
4. Percentages should be formatted consistently.
5. Currency values should use consistent currency formatting.
6. KPIs should respond correctly to dashboard filters and slicers.
7. KPI interpretation should consider the relevant business context.
8. Revenue should be clearly identified as merchandise revenue/GMV rather
   than profit.
9. Operational KPIs should use only delivered orders where appropriate.
10. KPIs should support management decisions rather than simply display
    numerical values.
